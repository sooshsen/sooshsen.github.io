Hi, I'm Sushmita Senapati!

I am neuroscientist from India, currently based in Germany.

I completed my B.Tech in Biotechnology from the IIT Roorkee, before joining the MSc program in Integrative Neuroscience at OvGU, Magdeburg. My MSc thesis project focused on characterizing network oscillation dynamics in a GABA-deficient genetic mouse model, mainly employing in vitro electrophysiology to obtain extracellular recordings. I also work on building a computational model of the hippocampal CA1 subregion to further understand the elements of the oscillatory drive in the same mouse model.

Outside the lab, I love expressing myself through painting and strumming on the guitar. Music and colors help me recharge and think in new ways. You’ll often find me watching clouds drift by like it’s serious business, bingeing a good anime series, or planning my next travel adventure.
